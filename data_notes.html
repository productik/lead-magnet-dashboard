Sources of truth

Everything on this dashboard comes from BigQuery project ik-marketing-data, read with SELECT queries only (nothing is written back). The queries run in the scripts below, and their results are saved as local files that the dashboard reads. Emails are turned into a one-way hash inside BigQuery before they leave, so no names, emails or phone numbers are stored locally.

How the tables are joined

From To Key Notes
Leads (2) Analyses (3) uuid Got a report; device
Leads (2) HubSpot (4) hubspot_id = contact_id Registrations, IK learner, new vs known. 25% of leads have no HubSpot ID and count as "not registered"
Leads (2) Sales (5) hubspot_id = Hubspot_Id, else hashed email
Clickstream (1) HubSpot (4) hs_id Only about 6% of visitors are identified
Tool clicks (6) Visits and registration forms (7) student_uuid = User_ID Same browser ID. The ikiq. subdomain gives visitors a different ID, so the registration-form link is weaker there
Registration forms (7) Sales (5) hashed email Sales are traced only for people who registered
Clickstream (1, 6) Backend (2, 3) none Different IDs, so page counts and backend counts are shown side by side, never mixed in one funnel

Check a number yourself

Paste these into the BigQuery console. Change the dates to the dashboard's period; it ends on the data date shown at the top. Small differences come from rows added after the last extract.

Leads per tool (matches "Leads captured"):

WITH leads AS (
  SELECT lead_magnet, LOWER(TRIM(email)) AS email, hubspot_id
  FROM `ik-marketing-data.Tech_ML_Projects.Resume Analysis - GQL data`
  WHERE DATE(created_at) BETWEEN '2026-09-17' AND '2026-10-16' AND NOT LOWER(email) LIKE '%test%'),
test_contacts AS (
  SELECT contact_id FROM (
    SELECT contact_id, test_lead, ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY contact_id ORDER BY row_added_at DESC) rn
    FROM `ik-marketing-data.Sales_Dashboard.contacts`
    WHERE contact_id IN (SELECT hubspot_id FROM leads)) WHERE rn = 1 AND LOWER(test_lead) = 'true')
SELECT lead_magnet, COUNT(DISTINCT email) AS people
FROM leads WHERE hubspot_id IS NULL OR hubspot_id NOT IN (SELECT contact_id FROM test_contacts)
GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 2 DESC

System processing time per tool:

SELECT lead_magnet, COUNT(*) AS runs,
  APPROX_QUANTILES(response_time_in_seconds, 100)[OFFSET(50)] AS typical_s,
  APPROX_QUANTILES(response_time_in_seconds, 100)[OFFSET(95)] AS slowest_5pct_s
FROM `ik-marketing-data.Tech_ML_Projects.Resume Analysis`
WHERE DATE(created_at) BETWEEN '2026-09-17' AND '2026-10-16' AND IFNULL(client_type, '') != 'sandbox'
GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 2 DESC

One person's webinar registration dates (put in a HubSpot ID):

SELECT DISTINCT latest_gql_date AS registered_on
FROM `ik-marketing-data.Sales_Dashboard.contacts`
WHERE contact_id = '<HubSpot ID>' AND latest_gql_date IS NOT NULL
ORDER BY 1

One person's purchases:

SELECT DATE(sale_date) AS sale_date, program_name, net_revenue
FROM `ik-marketing-data.Marketing_data_new_logic.Finance_Net_Revenue`
WHERE CAST(Hubspot_Id AS STRING) = '<HubSpot ID>' OR LOWER(TRIM(email)) = '<email>'
ORDER BY 1

All IK sales in a period (the base for "share of all IK sales"):

SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT CONCAT(IFNULL(CAST(Hubspot_Id AS STRING), LOWER(TRIM(email))), '|',
         CAST(DATE(sale_date) AS STRING), '|', IFNULL(program_name, ''))) AS sales
FROM `ik-marketing-data.Marketing_data_new_logic.Finance_Net_Revenue`
WHERE DATE(sale_date) BETWEEN '2026-07-19' AND '2026-10-16'

People clicking a button on a tool page (Tool funnels → Buttons; swap in the click ID and page):

SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT student_uuid) AS people
FROM `ik-marketing-data.Marketing_data_new_logic.all_clickstream_data`
WHERE ClickID = 'ra-result-cta-book-session'
  AND REGEXP_CONTAINS(LOWER(page_value_url), r'ikiq\.interviewkickstart\.com/resume-analysis/results')
  AND SAFE_CAST(timestamp AS INT64) >= UNIX_MILLIS(TIMESTAMP('2026-04-20'))

Unique visitors to a tool page (Tool funnels → Opened the tool, visits part):

SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT User_ID) AS visitors
FROM `ik-marketing-data.Marketing_data_new_logic.Leads_Click_history`
WHERE step IN ('s0', 's1') AND Lead_Created_Time >= TIMESTAMP('2026-04-20')
  AND REGEXP_CONTAINS(LOWER(Page_URL), r'interviewkickstart\.com/ats-resume-checker')

On-page funnel step counts (Overview funnel; one tool):

SELECT ClickID, COUNT(DISTINCT student_uuid) AS people
FROM `ik-marketing-data.Marketing_data_new_logic.lead_magnet_clickstream_master`
WHERE act_date BETWEEN '2026-09-17' AND '2026-10-16' AND lead_magnet_type = 'Salary Analyzer'
  AND business_unit != 'Internal - Non-Prod'
GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 2 DESC

Known gaps

  • Not tracked in BigQuery: ATS "Connect with FAANG experts", "Unlock my results", the job-description step, the ATS results and Jobs for you pages; the Mock Interview app on mi.dexity.com (permissions, video, completion); Salary "Unlock your salary potential" as its own button; AI Quotient Share and Copy link.
  • Webinar attendance comes from Zoom and starts on 23 Apr 2026; a person who joined with a different email than they registered with isn't matched.
  • Lead-magnet history starts on 18 April 2026, so "share of all IK sales" and "bought before" can't see earlier use.
  • Assumption to confirm: HubSpot latest_gql_date = date of a webinar registration.
  • After isn't because of: sales after a tool followed it; they weren't necessarily caused by it.
